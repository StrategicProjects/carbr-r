## carbr 0.1.0

- Primeira versão:
  [`car_catalog()`](https://strategicprojects.github.io/carbr-r/reference/car_catalog.md),
  [`car_dictionary()`](https://strategicprojects.github.io/carbr-r/reference/car_dictionary.md),
  [`car_summary()`](https://strategicprojects.github.io/carbr-r/reference/car_summary.md),
  [`car_municipalities()`](https://strategicprojects.github.io/carbr-r/reference/car_municipalities.md),
  [`car_properties()`](https://strategicprojects.github.io/carbr-r/reference/car_properties.md),
  [`car_property()`](https://strategicprojects.github.io/carbr-r/reference/car_property.md),
  [`car_at()`](https://strategicprojects.github.io/carbr-r/reference/car_at.md),
  [`car_tbl()`](https://strategicprojects.github.io/carbr-r/reference/car_tbl.md)
  e
  [`car_cite()`](https://strategicprojects.github.io/carbr-r/reference/car_cite.md).
- Dados das 27 UFs (cerca de 8,6 milhões de imóveis), atualizados toda
  semana.
- [`car_summary()`](https://strategicprojects.github.io/carbr-r/reference/car_summary.md)
  lê um resumo pré-calculado: totais nacionais em segundos.
- Guias: “Primeiros passos”, “Inconsistências do cadastro” e “Onde a
  análise está parada”.
